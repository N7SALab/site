The Obsidian Archive

A place of forgotten knowledge. Dust motes dance in the faint light. The air is thick with the scent of aged parchment and something else... something indefinable.

Whispers echo through the silent halls. Secrets hidden in plain sight. Only those who seek truly shall find. The answers are here, if you dare to look.

Seek the truth. The path is not always clear. Patience is key. Trust no one.
